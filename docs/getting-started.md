# Getting Started

## Create your account

Sign up at [growmate.gg](https://www.growmate.gg/) with Discord, Google, Microsoft or email. Every new account starts with **2,500 coins**.

Tip: link a second login method in Settings straight away, so you can still get in if you lose access to one of them.

## Spin the Daily Spin every day

Once per UTC day you get a free spin of the prize wheel, at [growmate.gg/daily](https://www.growmate.gg/daily) or with `/daily` in Discord. It pays coins, XP, seeds, boosters or exclusive pots. Spinning daily builds a streak that pays more the longer it runs, but missing a full day resets it to zero.

## Spend your first 2,500 coins wisely

1. A **grinder**, **rolling papers** and a **lighter**, so you can smoke your harvest for buffs.
2. **Seeds** from the daily shop rotation. Common and Uncommon seeds are cheap and perfect for learning.
3. Skip the casino until you have a steady income.

## Plant and keep plants alive

Free players have 6 plant slots and premium players have 9. Planting a seed pays XP and coins instantly.

Each plant has **Water** and **Nutrients** meters that drain over time. If either hits 0% the plant dies, so check in regularly or use an **Auto Waterer** and **Auto Feeder**, which each cover 48 hours.

Full walkthrough: [Getting Started guide](https://www.growmate.gg/guides/getting-started)

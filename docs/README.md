# Welcome to GrowMate

[GrowMate](https://www.growmate.gg/) is a free weed growing and strain breeding game you can play inside Discord or in any web browser. Your progress is shared between both, so you can water your plants from your phone and harvest them later in your server.

These docs are a quick player handbook. The full, always up to date guides live on [growmate.gg/guides](https://www.growmate.gg/guides).

## What you do in GrowMate

* **Grow.** Plant seeds in your tent, keep them watered and fed, and harvest flower when they finish.
* **Breed.** Cross two specific parent strains to create a rarer hybrid, climbing from Common to Mythic.
* **Collect.** Over 140 strains, rare pots, equipment and badges.
* **Trade.** Sell flower on the player marketplace or trade directly with other growers.
* **Team up.** Join a crew, complete crew challenges together and climb the crew leaderboard.

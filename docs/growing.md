# Growing and Plant Care

Plants grow in real time. Every strain has its own grow time, yield and rewards, which you can compare on the [strain database](https://www.growmate.gg/strains) or in the [free strain data](strain-data.md).

## The basics

* **Water** resets to 100% with one click and costs nothing.
* **Feeding** uses plant food from the shop and resets nutrients to 100%.
* Both meters drain steadily. You get a warning at 25%, and the plant dies at 0%.
* **Auto Waterer** and **Auto Feeder** stop the drain for 48 hours.

## Boosters

Boosters change how a plant develops and stack with each other. For example, Flower Power shortens grow time and Worm Dirt raises yield. See the [equipment guide](https://www.growmate.gg/guides/equipment) for the full list.

## Pots

Pots are cosmetic and collectible. Some can only be won from the Daily Spin or limited events.

## After the harvest

Harvested flower goes to your stash. Sell it, trade it, or smoke it for buffs. See [earning and selling](https://www.growmate.gg/guides/earning-selling) and [smoking and edibles](https://www.growmate.gg/guides/smoking-edibles).

# Breeding and Genetics

Breeding is how you climb from Common seeds to Mythic strains.

## Two genetic trees

Every strain belongs to the **Indica** tree or the **Sativa** tree. Each tree is a pyramid. 32 Common strains sit at the bottom, and each step up comes from breeding one specific pair from the tier below:

Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic

The Indica apex is **Indica Love** and the Sativa apex is **Sativa Supreme**. Strains from different trees can't be crossed, with one exception: Indica Love and Sativa Supreme together make a **custom Mythic** that you name yourself.

## How to breed

1. Plant two parent seeds that form a valid pair.
2. Grow both to at least 50% (the flowering stage).
3. Open your Tent and click **Breed**, then pick the hybrid.
4. Both parents are used up, and the new hybrid starts growing from zero.

Every hybrid has exactly one parent pair. For example:

| Parents | Result | Rarity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cherry Pie x Blue Dream | Cereal Milk | Uncommon |
| Cereal Milk x Gushers | Gelato | Rare |
| Gelato x Animal Mints | Girl Scout Cookies | Epic |
| Girl Scout Cookies x Mochi Gelato | Platinum Punch | Legendary |
| Platinum Punch x Purple Panty Dropper | Indica Love | Mythic |

Every recipe is on the [Genetics Chart](https://www.growmate.gg/strains/genetics), and the `parent_a` and `parent_b` fields in the [strain data](strain-data.md) list them in machine-readable form.

## What a Mythic costs

Reaching one Mythic from scratch takes 32 Common seeds and 31 breeds. You can skip tiers by buying rarer seeds from the shop or winning them on the Daily Spin.

Full guide: [Breeding guide](https://www.growmate.gg/guides/breeding)

# Crews

A crew is a team of players. Everything members do (harvests, breeds, XP) adds to the crew's place on the crew leaderboard.

* **Creating a crew** costs 50,000 coins. You choose a name, a 2 to 5 character tag and a description.
* **Roles:** Leader, Admin and Member. Treasury payouts are shared 1.5x, 1.25x and 1x.
* **Recruitment** is public (anyone can join) or private (players apply). Leaders and Admins can also send direct invites.
* **Crew challenges** are shared goals. Completing them pays coins into the crew treasury.
* **Crew chat** and an activity log keep everyone in sync.

You can only be in one crew at a time.

Full guide: [Crews guide](https://www.growmate.gg/guides/crews). Browse crews at [growmate.gg/crews](https://www.growmate.gg/crews).
